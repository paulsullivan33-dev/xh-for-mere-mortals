$ xh --ignore-stdin --auth bench:tape :18092/echo | jq -c '{authorization: (.headers[] | select(.[0]=="Authorization") | .[1])}'
{"authorization":"Basic YmVuY2g6dGFwZQ=="}
$ echo YmVuY2g6dGFwZQ== | base64 -d
bench:tape
(Basic auth is the user and password joined by a colon
 and base64-encoded — encoding, not encryption)

$ xh --ignore-stdin --auth-type bearer --auth TOKEN :18092/echo | jq -c '{authorization: (.headers[] | select(.[0]=="Authorization") | .[1])}'
{"authorization":"Bearer TOKEN"}
(the lab's token is the literal word TOKEN; the header is
 the word Bearer, a space, and the token exactly as given)

$ xh --ignore-stdin :18092/echo | jq -c '{authorization: ([.headers[] | select(.[0]=="Authorization")] | length)}'
{"authorization":0}
(no --auth, no Authorization header at all)
